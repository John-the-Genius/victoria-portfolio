About

Ten years on set,
rarely off schedule.

Victoria has spent over a decade managing production for the Nigerian entertainment industry, running day-to-day operations on long-running series, feature films, documentaries and stage productions. She's produced or managed more than twenty projects — including a Netflix series and international documentaries — and built a reputation for finishing on time and within budget while cutting costs and sharpening efficiency wherever she works.

Her range runs from budgeting and vendor negotiation to crew leadership, script vetting, location management and post-production handoff — with a social-media and audience-engagement instinct on the side. As producer, she's taken projects from idea to finished work in her own right, including the documentary Owode Yewa Town and the stage play Ooni Luwo. Years of community and welfare work outside the industry shape how she runs a set: calmly, and with people first.

She's built her production career at two of Lagos's leading production houses — Singing Tree Films, owned by Remi Vaughan-Richards, and Native Media Limited, owned by Rogers Ofime — and now runs her own venture, Vuzey Empire Ltd, a registered arts and media company. A trained care nurse of over a decade and a recently qualified phlebotomist, she brings that same hands-on care to set, stepping in with medical support wherever cast or crew need it.
